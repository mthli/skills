## ② What it means

The jobs report came in three times stronger than forecast, with July revised
from a loss to a gain and wages growing a touch faster. For the economy that
is good news; for a stock market that had rallied two days on the idea of
cheaper money it is a problem, because a strong labor market gives the Fed
less reason to cut. Bond yields (the interest rate the government pays;
higher = tighter money) rose across the curve, most at the short end, and the
long bond is back above the 5.25% line that has gated this book's adds since
08-19. The groups that suffer from higher rates — small caps, real estate,
utilities — are the ones gapping down; tech is flat, and chips are green
underneath it (AMAT +2.1%, SOXX +1.0%), the one premarket tell in the book's
favor. It is a tell (08-06, 08-10): the close grades it.

The structural backdrop now reads CAUTION *confirmed* — the market-health scan
printed it twice — but the cache is two days stale ⚠️: it re-ran this morning
on 09-02's bars because Yahoo had not finalized 09-03 at run time, so its
confirmation scores the two sessions before yesterday's broad repair (8 of 11
sectors green, the Nasdaq back over its shelf). What CAUTION means in plain
terms: the index sits near its high while only about half of all stocks are
above their 50-day average, and defensive groups (utilities, groceries,
pharma) have beaten growth for a month — a narrow market, not a broken one.
Under the rule promoted this morning, the two breadth gauges in today's exam
*are* the regime grade; the cache is the backdrop, not the verdict. The
